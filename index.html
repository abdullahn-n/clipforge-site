Short clips from long gaming videos, uploaded to your own YouTube channel

ClipForge is a personal desktop tool. It runs on your own computer, finds the highlight moments in a long gaming video or stream, renders them as vertical short clips with captions, and uploads the clips you approve to your own YouTube channel.

How ClipForge uses YouTube

ClipForge uses YouTube API Services. After you sign in with your Google Account and grant permission, ClipForge can:

  • upload videos to your YouTube channel, with the title, description, tags, visibility and publish time you chose;
  • read your channel's name, and the status and view counts of videos on your channel, so it can confirm an upload worked.

ClipForge does nothing else with your YouTube account. It cannot delete videos, change your channel, or read your comments or messages.

Your data stays on your computer

ClipForge has no server. Your sign-in token and your clips are stored only on the computer you run it on, and are never sent to the developer or anyone else. The Privacy Policy has the details, including how to remove ClipForge's access to your Google Account.

You choose what is posted

ClipForge shows you every clip before it is posted, and you choose which ones to upload. Clips flagged for music or other content that is likely to cause a copyright claim are held back until you approve them.
